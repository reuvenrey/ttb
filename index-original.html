A stop-motion musical adventure

In a world taken over by oppressive stone hands, a young girl and her new-found friend travel to the ends of their universe to take back the world that was once theirs.

Welcome to Town Name

Our story takes place in the fictional town of Town Name, a once thriving and diverse community. After the Helping Hands took over, this utopia became a stale promise of yesterday; Farms paved over for factory districts, playgrounds left to rot...

It’s as if the 70’s were just getting ready to end when the Hands came in and stuck a 50’s facade over them.

Amara

Lead, Town Name

Three years after giant stone hands descend from the sky to claim their dominion over humanity, young Amara achieves the impossible and frees herself from the strings that tied her to them.

After secretly growing and nurturing the last known flower on Earth, Little Ray, she vows to find a sanctuary where her fragile compassion can grow safely.

Toby

Protagonist, Town Name

In an effort to locate a safehaven, Amara unexpectedly teams up with the boy next door, Toby, teaching him to break out of his shell and fight for what’s right.

Together, they must set off on a perilous journey through abandoned airports, bustling factory districts, and hoards of the strung and controlled.

The Factory District

On their way, our two protagonists stumble into a Helping Hands factory district where they encounter Amp and Volt - two droids working for the Hands to keep the townspeople under their rule.

Amp & Volt

Villains, Planet Evil

This goofy pair works for the Helping Hands managing one of their many factory locations which have enslaved the people of Town Name. Amp calculates the work to be done, and Volt shocks the humans into submission.
