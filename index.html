About the Film

Ties That Bind follows a young Amara in a town taken over by oppressive Helping Hands, large stone hands which have attached themselves to every citizen via a set of marionette-like strings.

Plant-loving Amara frees herself from her strings and, along with her neighbor / sidekick Toby, travels to the end of her world to find a safe home for her plant, Little Ray.

Writer and Director: Jacob Painter

Producer: Jess Grace Garcia

An Inspiring Story

Ties That Bind is an important story with an important meaning. Amara and Toby represent the good folks of the world taking actions, whether small or large, to tackle the evil. Whether it's building a jetpack and flying to space, or voting, any action against bad people is one in the right direction.
