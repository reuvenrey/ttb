About the Film

In a dystopian world where humanity is puppeted by giant stone hands in the sky, a brave young girl escapes her strings to protect the planet’s last surviving flower.

Together with her floral companion and an unexpected friend, she must outrun mechanical nightmares and build back a once-thriving rebellion to sever the world’s ties and discover true freedom.

Written and directed by Jacob Painter

Welcome to

EVERGREEN

A once loved city that has been reimagined as the promise of yesterday...
It’s as if the 70’s were just getting ready to end when someone came in and slammed a 50’s facade over them.

Meet Amara

Our film's lead is a fearless and inventive spirit. With one string cut, she discovers the strength to fight against the Helping Hands.

Guided by her mother's love for plants and her brother's passion for engineering, Amara works to rebuild what the world has lost. Her journey centers on helping the last surviving flower, Little Ray, reach safety so it can grow again

Curious, clever, and resilient, she knows anything is possible if you just give it a chance!

Amara's Sidekick, Toby

Toby is quite good ad hide-and-go-seek, watching the strung-up world from the safety of his treehouse nextdoor to Amara. In an effort to hang onto the past he remembers, he spends most of his time watching reruns of his favorite TB show, Harmonious Rex and the Electrobites.

Despite being afraid of the world outside his backyard, Toby will do anything to help Amara.

The Helping Hands

These outer-wordly stone controllers have decended upon Evergreen and strung up the town.

Under the guise of providing uniformity, structure, and purpose, the Hands employ mechanical tyrants and puppet-like control over the town's citizens to mine resources and exploit the world they've conqured.

Original Music

Amara and Toby's adventure is told in part with a soundtrack of 7 original musical numbers, written by Jacob Painter and Leo Simpe-Asante.

The soundtrack is simultaneously intense and orchestral, but also simple and grounded and reflects the style of the animation.
