Meet Amara

Three years after giant stone hands descend from the sky to claim their dominion over humanity, young Amara achieves the impossible by breaking free from the strings that tied her to them. After secretly growing and nurturing the last known flower on Earth, Little Ray, she vows to find a sanctuary where her fragile compassion can grow safely. In an effort to locate a safehaven, Amara unexpectedly teams up with the boy next door, Toby, teaching him to break out of his shell and fight for what’s right. Together, they must set off on a perilous journey through abandoned airports, bustling factory districts, and hoards of the strung and controlled.
