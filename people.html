Meet the Creative Team

Jacob Painter

Writer & Director, Los Angeles

Jacob Painter is a Los Angeles-based production designer known for crafting visually rich, story-driven environments across film, television, and immersive media. A graduate of The Los Angeles Film School (2016), Jacob specializes in practical, stylized sets that blend theatricality with grounded detail.

His work often incorporates handcrafted elements, unconventional materials, and a strong narrative sensibility, bringing worlds to life with a distinct visual voice. With a background in set design and art direction, Jacob continues to collaborate with filmmakers and creators to shape spaces that support story, emotion, and character.

Leo

Songwriter, London

Born in south-east London in the mid 2000s, Leo has been surrounded by a rich creative scene for as long as he can remember. Growing up, taking a trip to the theatre and falling into magical faraway worlds through the power of storytelling changed his life, and he’s known he wanted to be a writer ever since.

He began writing poetry and plays aged six, when he also began classical training in piano and clarinet. Later, he taught himself the guitar, saxophone, drums, and singing.

Sara Stafford

Set Designer, Los Angeles

Born in the midwest, Sara Statfford studied Sequential Art at the Savannah College of Art and Design focusing on visual development for animation and video games. Sara is passionate about storytelling and world building, and her tool of choice is her trusty iPad which she uses to sketch out all of her ideas. Outside of the artistic mediums Sara loves to read and write and puts her heart and soul into everything she creates.
